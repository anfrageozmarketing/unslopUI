# Classic flows (checklist by family)

Use next to `flow-catalog.md`. Walk each family and mark every flow: fits, does not fit (`na` + reason), unsure. A flow is "classic" when users of similar apps expect it.

| Family | Flows |
|---|---|
| New user | Browsing tutorial · Creating account · Onboarding |
| Account | Editing profile · Deleting and deactivating account · Logging in · Logging out · Resetting password · Switching account |
| Commerce and finance | Adding to cart · Booking and reserving · Canceling order and refunding · Canceling subscription · Listing · Purchasing and ordering · Redeeming · Subscribing and upgrading · Transferring money |
| Social | Banning and blocking · Calling · Chatting and sending messages · Commenting and replying · Following and subscribing · Gifting · Giving feedback · Inviting teammates and friends · Joining and accepting · Leaving · Liking and upvoting · Muting · Referring friends · Registering · Reporting · Requesting · Reviewing and rating · Scheduling · Sharing |
| Content | Adding and creating · Archiving · Copying and duplicating · Deleting and removing · Drawing · Editing and updating · Favoriting and pinning · Filtering and sorting · Importing and exporting · Listening to audio · Logging and tracking · Marking · Moving · Publishing · Recording audio and video · Reordering · Saving to collection · Searching and finding · Selecting and choosing · Starting and completing · Taking photos · Uploading and downloading · Watching video |
| Misc | Changing language · Connecting and linking · Enabling and disabling · Setting up · Showing and hiding · Switching to dark mode · Switching view · Turning on and off · Verifying |

Rules of use:
- The most common in practice (add/create, edit, delete, upload/download, select, search, filter/sort, verify, share) are expected in almost every app. Check these first, including their states (empty, error, undo).
- Most apps need 10-20 of these. Do not force the rest: mark `na` with a reason.
- Name flows by the user's goal ("Passwort zurücksetzen"), not by the screen.
